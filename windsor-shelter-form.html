How women are counted

Counting rules
• Every saved form = one request.
• Same scrambled code = same woman (exact match).
• Similar but not identical details = flagged for staff review, never merged automatically.
• No consent = anonymous request, counted as a request but never as a confirmed unique woman.
• Categories follow HIFIS definitions, to be confirmed with City staff.

Where AI helps and where people lead

AI assists (behind the scenes)

Turns quick notes into clean fields · flags possible repeat callers · builds the dashboard · drafts reports, case notes and handovers.

People lead (always)

Answer every call · provide trauma-informed support · decide who gets a bed · confirm every match · approve every note and report.

Concept prototype for a class presentation. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Welcome Centre. Not connected to HIFIS, a live AI model or any shelter database; “AI” steps are simulated with simple rules. A real pilot would use the shelter’s nonprofit AI accounts and require privacy, consent, security, retention and City/HIFIS review.
